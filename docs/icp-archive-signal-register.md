# TAICO EV ICP Archive Signal Register

**Status:** Internal research only — not product, market, or customer evidence  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-29

## Purpose

This register preserves useful commercial leads from historical repository material without promoting them into the catalog-backed ICP matrix. It exists to reduce future research time, not to validate a product–ICP relationship.

The current [`ICP Candidate Matrix`](./icp-candidate-matrix.md) remains the only working matrix for the current TKMC product range. Its E1 entries are based on TAICO MC 2026 Catalog v1.3. Every entry below is E0 because the source is archived planning material, references a legacy model, or lacks current TAICO commercial confirmation.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Do not publish, sell from, or create CRM automation from an E0 signal.
2. Do not infer that a legacy model is equivalent to a current TKMC model because capacity or power happens to match.
3. Do not reuse historical deployment, revenue, adoption, ROI, market, or certification statements without an approved current source.
4. Promote a signal only after the commercial owner confirms the exact current product, target market, buyer role, supported configuration, and permission to use the claim.

## How to use an archive signal

1. Copy the signal into a research brief; do not edit the E1 candidate matrix yet.
2. Record the exact current TKMC product and technical source that supports the proposed use case.
3. Use the sales confirmation form in [`icp-candidate-matrix.md`](./icp-candidate-matrix.md) to identify buyer, user, influencer, target country, configuration, and commercial evidence.
4. The commercial owner decides whether the relationship may become E2. Only E2+ relationships can be considered for CRM segmentation; separate public approval is still required for website use.
